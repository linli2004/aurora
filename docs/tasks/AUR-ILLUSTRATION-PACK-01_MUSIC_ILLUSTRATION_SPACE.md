# AUR-ILLUSTRATION-PACK-01 — Music Illustration Space

## Stages

1. **AUR-ILLUSTRATION-01 — Illustration Space Architecture**
   - Replace the dominant album-card surface in Music Space with a large 2D illustration field.
   - Keep the left identity and Aurora Core as quiet annotations.
2. **AUR-ILLUSTRATION-02 — Procedural Dynamic Language**
   - Add deterministic Vortex, Particle Bloom and Ink Terrain templates.
   - Drive breathing, line drift and particles from semantic audio features without creating a spectrum UI.
3. **AUR-ILLUSTRATION-03 — Quiet Lyrics Layer**
   - Parse local sidecar LRC files.
   - Show one current line and one subdued next line above the progress trace.

## Product boundary

This pack delivers a deterministic, metadata-seeded illustration translation for the Demo/Alpha bridge. It does not claim semantic computer-vision interpretation of artwork. Artwork-aware scene understanding remains a later Intelligence task.
