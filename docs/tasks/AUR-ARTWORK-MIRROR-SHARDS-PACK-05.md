# AUR-ARTWORK-MIRROR-SHARDS-PACK-05

## Stage 1 — Clear mirror artwork

The album-derived 2D artwork is regenerated as a clean, high-contrast,
opaque mirror pane. The previous full-frame halftone haze is removed.

## Stage 2 — Artwork-derived shards

Only meaningful dark and edge features are sampled. Each shard keeps a tone
from the cover illustration and is rendered as a small irregular mirror
fragment rather than a square particle.

## Stage 3 — Pitch vector field

The mirror stays clear at all times. High-frequency energy pushes fragments
away from their original coordinates; falling energy slowly brings them back.
There is no always-on particle cloud above the image.
