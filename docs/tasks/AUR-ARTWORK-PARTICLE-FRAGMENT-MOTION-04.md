# AUR-ARTWORK-PARTICLE-FRAGMENT-MOTION-04

## Goal

Keep the current forward artwork pipeline, but change the visual behavior from
“a mostly fixed square illustration” to “an artwork-derived fragment field”.

## What changed

1. The intact 2D illustration now yields earlier to the particle layer.
2. Fragments no longer stay confined to the original square silhouette.
3. Fragment distance vectors grow from pitch / treble / transient energy.
4. Low energy slowly recomposes the field; high energy pushes it outward.
5. Even in low energy, fragments remain lightly suspended instead of freezing.

## Result

The artwork still comes from the detected album cover, but it behaves more like
a suspended explosion / reassembly field.
