# AUR-ARTWORK-PARTICLE-DYNAMICS-PACK-02 Review

The artwork is no longer treated as a static square image with a generic overlay.

The service now generates:

1. a higher-contrast monochrome 2D illustration;
2. a particle sample map derived from that specific illustration;
3. a stable focal point for outward motion.

MusicIllustrationSpace uses a fast attack and slow release envelope:

- high-frequency energy rising: particles explode outward;
- high-frequency energy falling: particles gradually return;
- pause or unavailable analysis: the artwork settles back into its complete form.

The current runtime exposes high-band spectral energy rather than a dedicated
fundamental-pitch tracker. The animation therefore uses high-frequency energy
as the immediate pitch-height driver while preserving the requested interaction
model.
